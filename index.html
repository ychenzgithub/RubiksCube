Basics基本知识

Colours颜色

A cube has six faces of different colours. The six centre pieces never move relative to each other, however you turn; only the 8 corner pieces and 12 edge pieces move.

魔方有不同颜色的六个面。其中六面的中心块是无论怎么转都保持不变的。转动时变化的只有 8 个角块和 12 个边块。

The colours of a standard cube are fixed: white is opposite yellow, red is opposite orange, and blue is opposite green.

标准魔方的颜色也是固定的：白面对着黄面，红面对着橙面，蓝面对着绿面。

White (bottom)白（朝下） Yellow (top)黄（朝上） Red红 Green绿 Orange橙 Blue蓝

For most steps it is convenient to hold the cube with white facing down and yellow facing up, with red, green, orange and blue around the sides. With yellow on top, looking down from above, the side colours run red → green → orange → blue counter-clockwise.

为了方便起见，在大多数操作时可以将白面朝下，黄面朝上，红绿橙蓝四面朝侧方。黄上白下时，侧面颜色（从上往下看）逆时针依次为红、绿、橙、蓝。

Notation标记方法

A solution is a sequence of turns, and each turn is written with a letter. The six faces are named after the first letter of their English names:

魔方解法由一系列旋转步骤组成，通常我们用字母来标记每一个步骤。六个基本面（一层）用英文单词的首字母大写表示：

R · LRight · LeftR (right) 右 · L (left) 左
U · DUp · DownU (upper) 上 · D (down) 下
F · BFront (the face toward you) · Back (the face away from you)F (front) 前，指面对自己的一面 · B (back) 后，指背对自己的一面

The basic move is a face turn: one face turns around its centre, clockwise as seen when looking straight at that face.

基本的旋转方法是面旋转，就是将一个面沿着其中心轴转动。转动可以顺时针（从面外向里看）或者逆时针。

RCapital letter: turn that face 90° clockwise.大写字母：顺时针转单个面 90 度。
R'A prime (') means counter-clockwise.字母后带 ' 就是逆时针转。
R2A 2 means turn 180°.字母后带 2 是旋转 180 度。
rLowercase: turn that face together with the layer behind it (two layers). On the 4×4 this guide uses a different convention — see the 4×4 chapter.小写字母跟大写对应字母类似，但是同时转同一面的两层。四阶魔方中小写字母含义不同，见四阶一章。
MThe middle layer between L and R, turned in the same direction as L.L 和 R 之间的中间层，转动方向与 L 相同。
x y zWhole-cube rotations: turn the entire cube 90° clockwise around the left–right, up–down and front–back axes (in the direction of R, U and F).体旋转（不太常用）：将整个魔方分别围着水平、竖直和前后轴顺时针转 90°（方向分别与 R、U、F 相同）。

3×3 Beginner method (layer by layer)三阶魔方层先法

This method finishes the bottom layer first, then the middle layer, then the top layer. It is a simplified version that stresses understanding each step and keeps the number of things to memorise as small as possible — it is the method I first used to teach my daughter.

本方法首先完成底层，然后完成中层，然后完成上层。该方法为简化版，强调了每一步的理解，最大限度地减少了需要记忆的数量。这也是我最初教女儿解魔方的方法。

Step 1 · White cross第一步：白十字

First hold the cube with the yellow centre on top and bring the four white edges up to the yellow face, forming a little "flower" around the yellow centre.

首先将黄中心块放在上方，把四个白边块转到黄面，形成一朵十字小花。

Then turn the top layer until one white edge's side colour matches the side centre below it, and turn that side 180° so the white sticker goes to the bottom (white) face. Repeat for every white edge to finish the white cross on the bottom (turn the cube over to check).

然后转动带有十字小花的上层，使得某一白色边块的侧面匹配侧面中心块颜色，将这个侧面下转 180 度，使边块白色一面转到底面（白面）。依次重复上述步骤，使得所有白边块转到底面，完成底面白十字（这时可以看看底面检查一下）。

A faster way更快的方法

Skip the flower and send each white edge straight to the bottom with white facing down. While doing so, keep the order of the four side colours in mind: seen from below, red → green → orange → blue clockwise. Look at where the white sticker of each edge is:

完成白十字有个更快的方法，就是不再首先将白边块放到黄色面，而是直接将它们转到底层，而且白面朝下方。在向下转的同时要注意白边块四种侧面颜色的相对位置，即顺时针（从下往上看）依次为红绿橙蓝。将白边块转到底层的方法是查看各目标边块的白面。若白面在：

  • On the bottom, but in the wrong place → turn it up to the top face, then continue.底面但位置不正确 → 转到顶面，继续。
  • On the side of the bottom layer → turn it up to the upper layer or the top face, then continue.下层 → 转到上层或顶面，继续。
  • On the top face → turn the top layer to the matching position, then turn that side down 180°.顶面 → 转顶层到对应位置，然后转到底层。
  • On the side of the upper layer → turn the top layer so the edge sits just to the right of its target, then do上层侧面 → 转顶层到对应位置右方，用
  • In the middle layer → turn the top two layers to a suitable position, then turn the edge down to the bottom.中层 → 转上中两层到适合位置，下转到底层。

When all four white edges are on the bottom, turn the bottom layer so the four side colours match their centres.

四个白边块都转到底层后，转动底层使四个侧面颜色与侧面中心块一致。

Step 2 · White corners第二步：白角块

Look for corners that contain white. If a white corner is in the upper layer with white facing sideways (not up), turn the top layer so the corner's other side colour lines up with its side centre, then use a wrench:

检查带有白色的角块。如果有白色角块在上层，且白面在侧方（不在上面），则转动上层使白色角块另一侧面对准目标侧面，然后用扳手：

  • White facing right →白面在右侧面 → (right wrench)（右扳手）
  • White facing left →白面在左侧面 → (left wrench)（左扳手）

If white faces up, first use a long wrench to move the white sticker to the side of the upper layer, then use the method above:

若白面在顶面，则先使用一下长扳手将白面移到上层侧面，再用上述白面在侧方的方法：

(right long wrench)（右长扳手）   (left long wrench)（左长扳手）

If the white corner is in the bottom layer (in the wrong place or twisted), use R U R' or L' U' L as appropriate to bring it to the side of the upper layer, then use the method above.

若白面在下层或底层，则酌情选择 R U R'（右扳手）或 L' U' L（左扳手）将白面移到上层侧面，再用上述白面在侧方的方法。

Step 3 · Middle layer第三步：中间层

Pick a middle-layer edge slot (the target), note the colours of the two centres on either side of it, and find the edge piece with those two colours.

选择侧面一棱（目标棱），检查棱两边中心块的颜色，然后找到带有这两种颜色的边块。

If that edge is in the upper layer, turn the top layer until the edge's side colour matches its side centre, and hold that centre facing you. Both algorithms start from this position:

若找到的边块在上层，则转动上层，使得该边块侧面颜色与侧面中心块一致。两个公式都基于已对号侧面面对自己的位置：

  • The edge needs to go to the left (it is to the right of its slot) — right return边块在目标棱右方（需要放到左边）— 右回归
  • The edge needs to go to the right (it is to the left of its slot) — left return边块在目标棱左方（需要放到右边）— 左回归
Mnemonic: “away, flip, back, back — pick the kid up from school.” Line the edge up with its side centre first, then: 1. turn the top layer to move the edge away from its slot; 2. flip the slot's side up (opposite direction to 1); 3. undo 1; 4. undo 2 — the edge now lines up with the corner below the slot; 5. move this aligned pair out of the way; 6. flip the slot's side up; 7. undo 5; 8. undo 6. 左右回归记忆口诀：“远切回回，接孩子放学”。先让目标块侧边对齐同色侧面，然后 1. 转动顶层将目标块移开远离目标位置；2. 上翻目标位置棱（与 1 相反方向）；3. 逆操作 1；4. 逆操作 2 使得目标块与目标位置底块对齐；5. 让开对齐的两块；6. 上翻目标位置棱；7. 逆操作 5；8. 逆操作 6。

If the edge you need is already in the middle layer (in the wrong slot or flipped), do the other slots first. If you get stuck, do one right return to pull the edge out to the top, then insert it properly with a second return.

若找到的边块在中层，则先做其他棱。如果无法继续，则先做一次右回归将目标块移出，再按照上述方法做第二次右回归让目标块归位。

Step 4 · Yellow cross第四步：黄十字

This step has two parts: first make a yellow cross on the top face without worrying about the side colours, then move the yellow edges so their side colours match the side centres.

本步骤有两小步，首先完成顶面黄块十字架而不考虑色块位置，然后调整黄边块位置使其侧面颜色与侧面中心块一致。

Make the cross十字

Look at the four edge stickers around the yellow centre on top.

查看顶面四个十字棱块位置的黄块。

Dot — only the centre is yellow点 — 只有中间黄块
Double-F formula: gives a line. Then continue with the line case.双 F 公式：得到一字型，再按一字型处理。
L shape — hold its corner at the front-rightL 型 — L 夹角朝向右下方

Line — hold it horizontally一字型 — 将一字横向放置

Single/double F: turn the F face clockwise (one layer = F, two layers = f), do the flowing R U R' U', then turn F back the same way. 单双 F 公式记忆：先将 F 面（单层 F 或双层 f）右转，然后做 R U R' U' 连贯公式，然后将 F 面同样返回。

Match the side colours调整

Turn the top layer so that as many yellow edges as possible match their side centres. If two adjacent edges are correct, hold them at the right (R) and back (B), then do the R formula, which swaps the front and left edges:

转动上层，使得黄边块侧面颜色与侧面中心块一致的个数尽量多。如果有两个相邻的侧面颜色已经正确，则将这两面放在 R（右）面和 B（后）面，做 R 公式（交换前面和左面的边块）：

If no two adjacent edges are correct (two opposite ones are), do the R formula once from any position first, then use it again as above.

如果没有两个正确相邻侧面，则先在任意位置做一次 R 公式满足要求。

R formula: the second half is like the first, except the U turn is doubled (U2).R 公式记忆：后部分与前部分类似，只是 U 转两个 90 度。

Step 5 · Yellow corners第五步：黄角块

Also two parts: first put the four corners in the right positions (ignoring how they are twisted), then twist them so yellow faces up.

本步骤也有两小步，首先将角块位置放好，无论朝向，然后将各块朝向调整好。

Position位置

Check which yellow corners are already in the right position (its three colours match the three centres around it, in any orientation). If exactly one is correct, hold the cube so that corner is at the front-right of the top layer and do the U formula. The other three corners cycle counter-clockwise; do it once or twice until all four are in place. In any other situation, do the U formula once from any position until exactly one corner is correct.

检查已有的黄色角块位置是否正确。若有一块正确，三块不正确，则转动整个魔方，使得位置正确块放在 R 面和 F 面相交棱（右前上角），做 U 公式使另外三个角块做逆时针交换。做一次或两次后其它三块会到正确位置。其它情况，则先任意做 U 公式使得黄色角块位置一块正确三块不正确。

U formula: the first half moves the back-right top corner down to the back-left bottom (the U turn first moves the correct corner out of the way); the second half brings the lined-up lower two layers back.U 公式记忆：前部分为把右后上方角块移到左后下角（需用 U 先移除对的角块），后部分为拉回对齐的下两层块。

Orientation朝向

Hold the cube so any corner with yellow not facing up is at the front-right of the top layer, and do the RD formula two or four times until that corner's yellow faces up. If other corners are still wrong, turn only the top layer to bring the next one to the front-right (don't move anything else), and repeat. The lower layers look scrambled in between; they come back once every corner is done.

检查黄角块的颜色朝向。将任意不正确块放在 RF 棱（右前上角），然后做 RD 公式两次或四次。若还有不正确块，继续转动上层将其放至 RF 棱（其它不要动），继续做上述口诀两次或四次。中间过程中下面两层看起来会被打乱，所有角块完成后会自动恢复。

Finger trick: rotate the whole cube 90° to the left around the front–back axis (z') and do U' R' U R instead — the same moves, easier to perform.RD 公式快速手法：可将魔方整体沿前后轴往左转 90 度（z'），然后用 U' R' U R 公式，更容易操作。

4×4 reduction method四阶魔方降维法

Each face of a 4×4 has 16 stickers, and there is no fixed centre piece. The reduction method first builds each face's four centre pieces into one big centre, then pairs up the two edge pieces of every edge so each pair acts as one big edge. The 4×4 is then “reduced” to a 3×3 and can be solved with 3×3 methods.

四阶魔方的每一面有 16 个色块，而且没有不动的一个中心块。我们可以用降维法来完成四阶魔方，也就是将每一面的中心四个方块拼成相同颜色，合并成一个大的中心块。然后将各棱的两个边块也按相同颜色组合对齐，将每一个组合视为一个大的边块。这样，整个四阶魔方就“降维”为三阶魔方，可以用三阶魔方的方法来求解。

4×4 notation: moves are written like on the 3×3, but here the lowercase letters l, r, u, d, f, b mean only the second layer from that face (an inner slice), not two layers together. Two layers together are written with a w, e.g. Dw = the bottom two layers. 四阶标记：四阶魔方转动手法的记录跟三阶魔方类似，用单字母来表示一次的转动。但是跟三阶魔方不同的是，小写字母 l、r、u、d、f、b 等代表某一面从外向里的第二层，而不是两层一起。两层一起用字母后加 w 表示，比如 Dw 代表下两层。
